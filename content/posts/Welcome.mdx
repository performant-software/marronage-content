The Marronage Mapping Project documents the locations and histories of maroon settlements — communities founded by people who liberated themselves from slavery — across the Caribbean and South America.

This site brings together settlement records, events, and primary sources into a searchable, map-centered interface. Use the **Places** tab to browse settlements, **Events** to follow the historical record over time, and **Paths** to walk through curated narratives that connect places.

Posts like this one share project updates, methodological notes, and short essays from the research team. We'll be publishing regularly as the dataset grows.
