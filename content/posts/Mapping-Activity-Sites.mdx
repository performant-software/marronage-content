Settlement records in the Marronage dataset fall into two categories. **Settlements** — fixed communities — have a known coordinate and appear as a single point on the map. **Activity Sites** — areas where maroon activity is documented but not localized to a specific village — appear as translucent circles whose radius reflects the documentary uncertainty for that place.

The radii are computed per region, since the geographic scale of "the area around" a place differs across the Caribbean coast, the Venezuelan llanos, and the highlands of Jamaica. We worked with the project's data editors to define a baseline for each polygon group, then derived per-record radii from there.

When a record has a certainty radius, the point marker is hidden so the circle reads cleanly against the basemap. Clicking inside the circle still opens the record's detail panel.

Future posts will cover the data sources behind each region's radius baseline and how the project decides between point and radius representation as new records come in.
